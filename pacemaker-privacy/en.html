Paceful Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 5, 2026
Last updated: June 5, 2026

1. Introduction

Paceful ("the App") is an iOS task management app designed for people who work irregular shifts. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your data.

The App is operated by an individual developer.

  • App name: Paceful
  • Operator: Shoma (individual developer)
  • Contact: kukubb13251@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

The App does not collect any personal information from users.

Specifically:

  • No account registration is required
  • We do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number
  • We do not transmit usage logs or activity history to any external service
  • We do not embed any advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs
  • We do not collect device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV)
  • We do not collect location information

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

All data created in the App is stored exclusively on your device.

  • Tasks, shifts, and Thought Bucket contents: stored locally in SwiftData
  • App settings: stored locally in UserDefaults
  • iCloud sync: not used in this version
  • Transmission to any external server: never

When you uninstall the App, all this data is deleted.

4. Sharing With Third Parties

The App does not share user data with any third party. Since we do not collect data in the first place, there is nothing to share.

5. Advertising

The App displays no advertisements and does not embed any advertising network SDKs.

6. Crash Reports and Diagnostics

The App does not implement any custom crash reporting.

Apple's built-in iOS systems may collect crash logs and diagnostic data. This is governed by Apple's privacy policy and your device settings ("Settings" → "Privacy & Security" → "Analytics & Improvements"). You can disable this collection at any time.

7. Access to Photo Library

The App may request access to your photo library so that you can attach images to entries in the Thought Bucket feature.

  • Only the photos you select are retrieved (using PHPickerViewController; we do not request full library access)
  • Selected images are compressed on-device to 1024px / JPEG quality 0.8 before being stored
  • Compressed images are stored only in the App's local database and are never transmitted externally
  • Deleting a Thought Bucket entry also removes its image data from your device

8. Notifications

The App uses local notifications only.

  • Reminders (gentleNudge) are generated and delivered entirely on your device
  • No push notification server is used
  • You can enable or disable notifications at any time

9. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for an age rating of "4+". Because the App does not collect any personal information, it does not collect information from children either.

10. Your Rights

Because the App stores your data only on your device, you can access, modify, and delete your data entirely within the App.

  • Data deletion: delete tasks within the App, or uninstall the App
  • Data export: not supported in this version (under consideration for future releases)

11. Contact Us

For questions about this policy or the App's privacy practices, please contact:

kukubb13251@gmail.com

12. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated without prior notice. When changes are made, the updated date will appear at the top of this page. Material changes will also be announced in the App Store "What's New" section.
